## About

I design and ship **AI and data systems that work in African public-interest settings**: newsrooms fighting misinformation, health programmes that need reliable evidence, and civic platforms that connect citizens with institutions.

My background is deliberately hybrid. I am trained as an **epidemiologist and biostatistician**, I build **production-grade full-stack applications**, and I apply **NLP and machine learning** with a strong bias for rigorous evaluation, honest limitations and human oversight. Much of my work focuses on the Lake Chad Basin, with the aim of building durable evidence ecosystems for Central and West Africa.

**Currently**

- 🛰️ **Code for Africa AI for Good Fellow** — technical lead on [SaoCheck AI](https://github.com/P-Adjinsala/saocheck-ai), an AI-assisted fact-checking platform built with WenakLabs in Chad.
- 📊 **Health Data Analyst & MEL Specialist** at MediX Consulting.
- 🌍 **Full-Stack Developer & Webmaster** at AfricTivistes CitizenLab Cameroun.
- 🎓 **Lecturer in database systems** at ISTEM and IUSTY.

## What I do

| | Focus | Typical deliverables |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| 🤖 | **Applied AI & NLP** | Claim matching, text classification, model evaluation with grouped cross-validation, bias and dataset audits, decision-support AI with explicit reliability thresholds |
| 🧱 | **Full-stack engineering** | Typed React front ends, FastAPI and Node.js APIs, PostgreSQL schemas with migrations, role-based access, automated tests |
| 📈 | **Epidemiology & biostatistics** | Survey and programme data analysis (DHS, MICS6, PASEC), multilevel models (HLM), propensity score matching, survival analysis |
| 🗂️ | **Data systems & MEL** | Data collection pipelines (KoboToolbox, ODK), indicator frameworks, dashboards and reporting for decision-makers |
| 🛡️ | **Civic tech & information integrity** | Fact-checking workflows, civic participation platforms, digital rights and open data advocacy |
